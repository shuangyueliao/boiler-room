YOU ARE THE DUTY ENGINEER. The main boiler makes steam; three engines await exact pressure.
· Tap a valve wheel to route steam (gates open/shut, three-ways pick line A, B, or both).
· Set the furnace low / medium / high — high fire fills fast but stresses the plant.
· Hold each engine inside its target band to fill the work orders before the shift ends.
· Over 90 on an engine bursts the pipe; over 100 on the boiler blows the roof. Keys: 1-6 valves, Q/W/E furnace, M sound.
